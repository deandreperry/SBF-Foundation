Board Leadership

Serve with strategic leadership and purpose.

Serve as a board member and help guide the mission, strategy, and long-term success of Social Butterflies Foundation. Board members provide governance, ensure fiscal responsibility, and champion our work in the community.

Board Responsibilities

  • Attend quarterly board meetings
  • Participate in strategic planning
  • Support fundraising efforts
  • Serve on at least one committee
  • Act as an ambassador for the foundation

Ideal Candidates: Individuals with leadership experience, a passion for serving others, diverse professional expertise, and a commitment to advancing the mission of Social Butterflies Foundation.

Download and complete the board member application, then email it to admin@mysbf.org.

Become an Advocate

Use your voice to raise awareness and create change.

Advocacy is about making invisible illness visible and fighting for better care, research, and understanding.

Ways to Advocate

  • Share your story on social media
  • Participate in awareness campaigns
  • Contact legislators about important issues
  • Support the DMV License Plate Campaign
  • Educate your community about lupus and fibromyalgia
Join the Movement

Your Voice Matters

Every story shared, every conversation started, and every letter sent creates ripples of change. Together, we can transform how society views and supports those with invisible illnesses.

Advocacy does not require expertise, just passion and the willingness to speak up.
