About the Scholarship

Helping students spread their wings.

The Chastity Corbett Youth Scholarship is offered by Social Butterflies Foundation to graduating seniors in Virginia living with lupus or fibromyalgia, or those with a biological parent or grandparent diagnosed with either condition, who plan to pursue higher education.

Named in honor of SBF founder Chastity Corbett—known as The Lupus Conqueror—the scholarship celebrates resilience, determination, and the power of community support. It reminds students that lupus and fibromyalgia are words, not sentences, and that their educational goals remain within reach.

“Butterflies don’t let each other fly alone.” — Chastity Corbett

2027 Scholarship Cycle

Eligibility at a Glance

  • Reside in Virginia and attend a Virginia high school
  • Be a graduating high school senior
  • Maintain a minimum cumulative 3.0 GPA
  • Be accepted to an accredited college, university, or post-secondary institution and plan to enroll in Fall 2027
  • Be affected by lupus or fibromyalgia through a personal or immediate family diagnosis
  • Demonstrate financial need
