About the Collection

More Than Custom Apparel - It's a Movement

This isn't just apparel - it's personal.

The SBF Custom Collection was created for those who carry strength, resilience, and purpose every single day. Whether you're living with lupus or fibromyalgia, supporting someone who is, or standing in solidarity with the community, every piece represents a story that deserves to be seen.

Each item is fully customizable, allowing you to add your name, your number, and your identity. Because this isn't about wearing a brand - it's about wearing what you stand for.

Crafted with premium materials, detailed stitching, and performance-quality construction, every piece is built for comfort, durability, and impact.

Every purchase helps support lupus and fibromyalgia awareness, programs, and community initiatives through Social Butterflies Foundation.

This is more than what you wear. This is how you represent the fight.

Before You Order

Custom order, shipping & return information

Return, Refund & Order Policy

All SBF custom apparel is made-to-order and personalized specifically for each customer. All sales are final once an order is placed.

Within 15 days of delivery, replacements or refunds may be available for defective, incorrect, or materially different items.

Incorrect size selection, customer-submitted customization errors, and changes of mind are not eligible for return or refund.

Shipping & Processing

Processing time is 7-9 business days. Standard U.S. shipping is typically 8-10 business days; expedited shipping is typically 3-5 business days after processing.

Standard shipping is $9.95. Expedited shipping is $35. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the order is incorrect.

Sizing & Customization

Please review the size chart carefully. Measurements reflect garment dimensions, and customers are responsible for size, name, number, and spelling selections before placing an order.
