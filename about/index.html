About Social Butterflies Foundation

Support, education, advocacy, and care for every thriver.

Social Butterflies Foundation empowers individuals affected by lupus and fibromyalgia through education, advocacy, direct support, and community-driven programs that help thrivers navigate their journey with strength and hope.

Message From Our Founder

Our Story

Born from lived experience.

Social Butterflies Foundation was born from lived experience. After facing the challenges of lupus and fibromyalgia firsthand, founder Chastity Corbett recognized the need for greater support, education, advocacy, and practical resources for individuals navigating chronic illness. What began as a personal journey grew into a mission-driven organization dedicated to ensuring no one faces these conditions alone.

Our Advocacy Commitment

Awareness is not a month—it is a movement.

At Social Butterflies Foundation, we believe lupus and fibromyalgia awareness is not a month—it’s a movement.

These conditions do not impact individuals for only a few weeks each year. They affect thrivers every day, which is why advocacy, education, and support must continue every day as well.

We encourage every thriver, caregiver, supporter, and advocate to use their voice, share their story, educate others, and help create meaningful change in their communities. Together, we can increase awareness, reduce stigma, improve access to resources, and ensure that no one faces lupus or fibromyalgia alone.

“Lupus and Fibromyalgia Awareness is not a month, it’s a movement. We do not live with these illnesses for just a month. Therefore, I advocate and encourage all thrivers to advocate every single day.”

— Chastity Corbett, Founder & Executive Director

What We Do

Programs built around real needs.

Support Groups

Safe, compassionate spaces where thrivers connect, share experiences, and support one another.

Financial Assistance

Emergency support to help cover medical expenses, medications, and essential needs.

Scholarships

Educational scholarships that help youth impacted by lupus and fibromyalgia pursue their goals.

Summit & Health Fair

A powerful gathering offering education, health screenings, resources, and community support for thrivers and their families.

Outreach

Providing essential resources—including wigs and CARE packages—to restore confidence, dignity, and hope in the communities we serve.

Education

Workshops, resources, and events that empower thrivers to better understand, manage, and advocate for their health.

Advocacy

Raising awareness and driving change through campaigns and community initiatives.

Mission Statement

Our Mission

To empower individuals affected by lupus and fibromyalgia through support, education, advocacy, and direct assistance—improving quality of life and ensuring no one faces these conditions alone.

Vision Statement

Our Vision

We envision a world where lupus and fibromyalgia are widely understood, properly supported, and no longer faced in silence—where every thriver has access to the resources, care, and community they deserve.
