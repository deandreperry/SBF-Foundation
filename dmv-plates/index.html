Official State Recognition

Bringing visibility across Virginia.

We are working with a local State Delegate to establish official awareness license plates for the state of Virginia, bringing visibility to lupus and fibromyalgia across our communities.

Featuring the purple awareness ribbon, these specialty plates help make invisible illness visible while supporting programs that serve those living with lupus and fibromyalgia.

  • Lupus Plate Goal: The state requires 450 Lupus Awareness Plate pre-orders before the plate can become a reality.
  • Your Impact: Once approved, the state will donate $15 from every plate purchased through the DMV.

How to Pre-Order Your Plate

Help bring the Lupus Awareness Plate to life.

1

Complete the Order Form

Visit the link below to submit your pre-order. Select “personalized plate” if you are transferring an existing plate or requesting a custom plate.

  • Lupus Awareness Plate: non-personalized is free
  • Lupus personalized or transferred plates are $10
  • Fibromyalgia non-personalized plates are $25
  • Fibromyalgia personalized plates are $35
Pre-Order Now
2

Complete DMV Registration Form

After submitting your order, you will receive an email with the official DMV registration form. You can complete and email it back or mail it to:

  • Social Butterflies Foundation
  • P.O. Box 1652
  • Newport News, VA 23601
Download Lupus Plate Form Download Fibromyalgia Plate Form
3

Pre-Orders Submitted to the State

Once we reach 450 pre-orders, a bill is presented to the Virginia General Assembly, the Governor signs off on the plate, and all pre-orders are submitted to the DMV for processing.

4

Receive Your Plate

Once approved and processed, the DMV will mail your official Lupus Awareness license plate directly to you.
