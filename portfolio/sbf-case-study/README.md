# SBF portfolio case study

Open `index.html` through the repository's local preview server at `/portfolio/sbf-case-study/`.

This is a standalone portfolio presentation. It does not change the SBF production pages. Its two embedded previews load the actual local Custom Apparel and Resource Center implementations; their external links lead to the live services. Images and fonts require internet access.

## Editorial framing

Title: **Designing Both Sides of Support**

Portfolio teaser: **Connecting merchandise discovery and resource access through a consistent visual identity for Social Butterflies Foundation.**

Interview introduction:

“My SBF work connects two distinct visitor needs: expressing support through merchandise and finding support through resources. I used the same brand foundation across both, but changed the information hierarchy to fit the task. The apparel page foregrounds products and collection discovery; the Resource Center organizes education and assistance by topic. My strongest design decisions are the category structure, repeated card anatomy, and clearer action destinations. The next step is testing discovery and handoffs, particularly the long resource page and the transition from catalog cards into the store.”
