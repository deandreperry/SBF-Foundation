About SBF

Who we are and why we exist.

Social Butterflies Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to empowering individuals impacted by lupus and fibromyalgia through compassionate support, direct assistance, and transformative programs. We provide life-changing resources, foster community, and raise awareness for these often-invisible illnesses.
