Eight Years of Impact

Explore our programs ↗

Compassionate support, practical resources, and a community that understands. These are some of the ways SBF stands alongside thrivers and their families.

01 / CONNECTION

Support Groups

A safe, compassionate space for youth, adults, families, and caregivers to connect, share, and support one another.

02 / ASSISTANCE

Emergency Financial Assistance

Limited assistance for qualifying Virginia residents with medical bills and prescription costs related to lupus and fibromyalgia.

03 / COMMUNITY

CARE Outreach Program

Support through life's challenges and meaningful moments, with community outreach including essential items and school supplies.

04 / OPPORTUNITY

Youth Scholarships

Educational support for qualifying Virginia graduating seniors living with lupus or fibromyalgia, or with an affected parent or grandparent.
