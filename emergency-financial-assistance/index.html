Emergency Financial Assistance

Living with lupus or fibromyalgia can create significant physical, emotional, and financial challenges. Medical expenses, missed work, and unexpected emergencies can make it difficult to meet essential financial obligations.

Social Butterflies Foundation (SBF) provides limited emergency financial assistance to eligible individuals living with lupus and/or fibromyalgia. Our goal is to help reduce financial hardship while supporting the health and well-being of those we serve.

Who May Apply

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for consideration, applicants must:

  • Be a Virginia resident age 18 or older.
  • Have a medically verified diagnosis of lupus and/or fibromyalgia.
  • Demonstrate an emergency financial need related to their medical condition.
  • Have exhausted other available assistance resources, including community organizations, government programs, and applicable insurance coverage.
  • Have not received EFA assistance from SBF within the previous 12 months.
  • Provide all required documentation to verify eligibility and financial need.

Program Policies

Assistance Guidelines

Maximum Assistance

Up to $250 per eligible person within a 12-month period, subject to available funding.

Direct Payments

Approved assistance is paid directly to the service provider or agency. Payments are not issued to applicants.

Zero-Balance Requirement

SBF's approved payment must resolve the remaining eligible balance. If the amount owed exceeds SBF's approved contribution, the difference must be paid through personal funds or other verified resources before SBF issues payment.

Available Resources

Applicants must seek assistance through other available programs and resources before requesting SBF assistance.

Funding Availability

Assistance is not guaranteed. All requests are reviewed individually based on eligibility, documentation, demonstrated need, and available funding.

Qualifying Needs

Eligible Expenses

Assistance may be considered for:

  • Medical bills directly related to lupus and/or fibromyalgia.
  • Medically necessary prescription medications.
  • Essential utilities, including electricity, water, or gas, when interruption of service could negatively affect the applicant's medical condition.
  • Other qualifying medical-related emergencies, subject to review and approval.

Expenses Not Covered

Rent, mortgages, vehicle payments, cell phone bills, cable television, and internet services.

Before You Apply

Required Documentation

Applicants must submit:

  1. Medical Diagnosis Verification

    Documentation confirming a lupus and/or fibromyalgia diagnosis. Acceptable documentation includes a healthcare provider letter, MyChart or other patient portal records, or a medical visit summary.

  2. Proof of Identity and Virginia Residency

    A valid identification document and documentation confirming current Virginia residency.

  3. Current Bill or Statement

    A document showing the provider's name, outstanding balance, and payment deadline.

  4. Documentation of Other Assistance

    Available records showing assistance requested, approved, denied, or received from other sources, including payments made toward the outstanding balance.

Additional documentation may be requested during the review process.
